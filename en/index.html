Comparative mediation and ADR law, worldwide

Since 2011, mediaresenzaconfini has tracked how mediation, conciliation, arbitration and ADR are regulated, practised and reformed around the world. It is edited by Carlo Alberto Calcagno, an Italian lawyer and certified family mediator engaged in long-term comparative research on mediation legislation. Most of the site is in Italian; the sections below give English-language readers direct access to the parts built for international use.

197 jurisdictions indexed · 190+ countries with legal texts translated into English, French, Spanish and Italian · 15 years of archive · updated continuously.

Start here

  • World legislation index — an alphabetical map of mediation and ADR legislation across 197 jurisdictions, from EU member states to the rest of the world.
  • Legal texts in force (multilingual) — the current mediation law of each country, in the original language plus English, French, Spanish and Italian translations, with sources cited.
  • Pending legislation tracker — mediation and ADR bills currently moving through national legislatures, updated as they progress.
  • EU countries compared — the 27 EU member states measured against the European Commission's EU Justice Scoreboard: efficiency, quality, independence and use of alternative dispute resolution.

About the translations

Legal texts on this site are reproduced in their original language together with translations produced with AI assistance. These translations are unofficial: they are not certified and have no legal value. Only the official texts published by the competent authorities of each country, referenced in the "Source" section of each page, are authoritative.

About the author and the underlying research

This site draws on ongoing comparative-law research by Carlo Alberto Calcagno covering mediation legislation across approximately 200 jurisdictions. Read more on the About & methodology page.
